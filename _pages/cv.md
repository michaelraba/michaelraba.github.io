## Summary

Mechanical engineer with an MSc from the University of Kentucky (December 2025) and project experience in finite element analysis,
acoustic simulation, material modeling, machine design and closed-loop controls, backed by strong numerical and programming skills.

## Education

**University of Kentucky**, Lexington, KY

- MSc, Mechanical Engineering, December 2025. Project: *POD Analysis of Turbulent Pipe Flow and Relaminarization*
- BA, Mathematics, May 2019
- Additional undergraduate mechanical engineering coursework (Fall 2026): Machine Design (ME 344), Controls (ME/AER 440)
- Relevant coursework: Machine Design using ANSYS Workbench (ME 514), Engineering Acoustics, Plasticity

## Experience

**Wayfinding Assistant**, UK HealthCare, Chandler Medical Center &mdash; July 2023 to present (also 2019&ndash;2020)

- Built a wayfinding map web app (Flask, Python, SQL) to route hospital visitors.
- Built a desktop lookup tool (AutoHotkey v2, HTML interface) that fuzzy-matches text copied from the health record against the department phone directory and ranks the closest matches.

**Graduate Research and Teaching Assistant**, University of Kentucky &mdash; 2020 to 2023

- Turbulence research with 11 PhD researchers at the University of Maryland and the University of Oxford; attended the AIAA Aviation Forum (2022).
- Taught ME 311 Experimental Design II (design of experiments, statistics in R), ME 330 Fluid Mechanics I and ME 321 Thermodynamics II recitations.

- **Simulation / CAE:** ANSYS Workbench (Static Structural, Acoustics, nonlinear materials, optimization), SIDLAB, FEA
- **CAD:** Rhino
- **Controls / hardware:** Simulink, Arduino, servo actuation, time-of-flight sensing
- **Programming:** MATLAB, Python (NumPy, Plotly, Flask), C++, Fortran, SQL, R, Linux, LaTeX
- **Methods:** static and fatigue design (Shigley), reduced-order modeling (POD), constitutive modeling, design of experiments
